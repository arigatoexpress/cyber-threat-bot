# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in `cyber-threat-bot` a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still correcting unsafe or inaccurate work.
- Keep discussion focused on defensive security, reproducible evidence, and
  practical user outcomes.
- Accept maintainer decisions when a change raises safety, legal, or
  operational concerns.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting exploit payloads, credential material, or instructions that turn a
  defensive workflow into offensive misuse.
- Derailing issues or PRs with unrelated promotion, spam, or personal disputes.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers at
[aristotlespec@gmail.com](mailto:aristotlespec@gmail.com). Maintainers may
remove comments, close issues, reject PRs, or restrict participation when needed
to keep the project safe and useful.

The full Contributor Covenant v2.1 is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
