# Go-To-Market

This repo can be sold as a virtual threat desk, not just a feed reader.

## Wedge

Sell a fixed-scope service that turns live threat data into fast, decision-ready action:

- `48-Hour Exposure & Patch Sprint`
- `Identity Boundary Review`
- `Agent Guardrail Assessment`

The trick is speed, specificity, and executive-ready output. Most companies do not want raw intel; they want to know whether their stack is exposed and what to do next.

## ICP

- AI startups shipping agents, copilots, or workflow automations
- B2B SaaS teams with lean security headcount
- MSSPs or vCISO shops that need white-label threat research

## Offer Ladder

1. Pilot
   - Price: $2.5k to $5k
   - Deliverable: one threat brief plus one exposure memo
2. Sprint
   - Price: $7.5k to $20k
   - Deliverable: validation, mitigation review, executive summary, customer-safe language
3. Retainer
   - Price: $15k to $30k per month
   - Deliverable: recurring monitoring, bespoke briefs, escalation support, and sales enablement

## Million-Dollar Path

- 20 pilots at $5k = $100k
- 20 sprints at $15k = $300k
- 5 retainers at $10k/month for 12 months = $600k
- Total = $1.0M

## First Sales Motion

- Run `threat-bot offers --profile profiles/ai-saas.json`.
- Pick the top 3 offers and turn them into outbound hooks.
- Use the generated buyer, price, and delivery plan as your initial pitch structure.
- Deliver one excellent paid pilot fast and turn it into a case study.
